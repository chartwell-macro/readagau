## Farm Productivity

The supported workbook contains 5,750 reported and canonical observations:
3,926 conventional, 1,680 climate-adjusted and 144 interim-adjustment rows.
All 19 conventional and seven climate-adjusted table families are classified.

## Historical Forecast Database

The package supports landing-page discovery, immutable download/cache,
required-field validation and package-owned normalisation. A local CSV or
workbook can also be supplied.

## Support policy

A discovered page is not automatically supported. Support requires exact
assets, raw-cell goldens, full counts, zero unexplained payload, canonical
validation, cache replay, benchmarks and independent review.
